Privacy Policy

Auction Day: Cricket Manager · Last updated 16 October 2026

Auction Day is a single-player cricket management game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your saved career is stored only on your own device, so you can continue where you left off. It never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes the save.

Internet access

The game works fully offline and does not send data over the internet.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that use data, such as online leagues, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
